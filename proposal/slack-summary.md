Proposed focus: a Bluesky friend leaderboard built on Crosshare.

The problem: "After I finish today's mini, I want to see how people I follow on Bluesky did on that same puzzle, without finding everyone's posts or building a separate friend list."

We'll reuse Crosshare's crossword experience. Our addition is Bluesky sign-in, publishing results as AT Protocol records, comparing times with people you follow, and sharing a spoiler-free result back to Bluesky.

For the three-week MVP, start with curated daily minis. Defer puzzle generation and new creation tools. First milestone: solve a seeded puzzle and publish a real AT result; next, connect the friend leaderboard and sharing.
